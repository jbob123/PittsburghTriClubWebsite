Club By-Laws

Amended and Restated Bylaws of the Pittsburgh Triathlon Club — adopted May 1, 2009.

Article I — General

The club's purpose is the amusement and recreation of its members, specifically the promotion of triathlon and multisport in Western Pennsylvania, operating under IRC Section 501(c)(7) tax-exempt status.

Article II — Membership

  • Single membership class, open to anyone interested in triathlon/multisport.
  • Annual dues determined by unanimous director vote.
  • Minimum of six member meetings annually.
  • Membership may be revoked for non-payment or misconduct at club events.
  • Members vote on director/officer elections; quorum is 50%. Proxy voting is permitted.

Article III — Board of Directors

  • Directors manage club business and affairs.
  • Annual regular meetings required; special meetings may be called by the president or two directors, with 10 days' written notice.
  • Majority director action governs decisions; vacancies are filled by member vote.
  • Directors serve without compensation but may be reimbursed for expenses.

Article IV — Officers

President, Vice-President, Secretary, and Treasurer, elected by members. No salary; expense reimbursement is allowed.

Articles V–VIII

Cover interested-director transactions, financial management, a fiscal year starting in January, and bylaw amendments — which require a majority director vote.
