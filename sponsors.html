Our Sponsors

Our sponsors support the performance and health of our athletes by providing quality products and services, often at a substantial discount. Many of our sponsors are athletes themselves! In turn, we support them with our business, our feedback, and our referrals.

Sponsor discounts are a member-only exclusive and subject to verification of eligibility. Please be prepared to provide your name and show a valid I.D. Discount codes are provided to club members via the club’s monthly newsletter. Not seeing it? Email pittsburghtriathlonclub@gmail.com.

Race Sponsors

Discounts on race registration are exclusively available to club members for selected races in the region. Historically, race discounts have been offered by the Race Directors for the Mighty Moraine Man, Janney and the Y North Park Triathlon, Presque Isle Triathlon, Steelman Triathlon, and Tri-CLE Rock Roll Run. Discount codes are provided to current club members via the monthly newsletter or direct email communications.

Become a Sponsor

Interested in sponsoring PTC, or want to recommend a sponsor? Here's what guides our partnerships, from the club's Sponsorship Guidelines:

  1. Sponsors should offer products or services relevant to the athletic nature and pursuits of PTC membership.
  2. Sponsors should offer products or services that benefit all members regardless of gender, athletic ability, etc.
  3. The club reserves discretion to decline proposals that don't meet its standards.
  4. Unless otherwise negotiated, sponsorship is non-exclusive — PTC may work with multiple sponsors in the same industry.
  5. The club may limit sponsor representation by industry, geography, or tier to avoid oversaturation.
  6. Sponsors can contribute via discounted offerings, donated goods/services, or financial support.
