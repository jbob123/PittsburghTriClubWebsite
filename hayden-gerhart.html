Coaching Philosophy and Approach

Every athlete is different. There is no “cookie cutter” program in endurance sports. I firmly believe that any person can set the goal to get across the finish line, then achieve it through hard work and discipline. I love establishing real connections with my athletes. I often say, “training fits into life, not the other way around.” Daily stressors certainly pile up and lead to a very adaptive program design; one that is highly specific to each athlete’s lifestyle, availability, and skill level.
