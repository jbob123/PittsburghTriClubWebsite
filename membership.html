IRONMAN TriClub Program

PTC is an official IRONMAN TriClub, which means members get:

  • Early entry to IRONMAN and IRONMAN 70.3 events
  • Priority, tiered race pricing
  • Deferral and payment plan options
Club Governance

Annual Board Elections

PTC is run entirely by member volunteers. Every dues-paid member is eligible to run for the Board of Directors.

Open Positions

  • President
  • Vice President
  • Secretary
  • Treasurer

Eligibility: any dues-paid PTC member can run for a Board position — you must have dues paid for the year you'd be serving.

Key Dates

Nov 15Deadline to declare candidacy
Nov 25Platform & bio submission deadline
Dec 1Voting opens
Dec 15Voting closes
Early JanResults announced

Voting happens online via a member email link, administered by two non-board volunteers to keep it confidential. To declare candidacy, contact the board at pittsburghtriathlonclub@gmail.com.
