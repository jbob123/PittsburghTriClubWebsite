Resources

Whether you're getting started on your multisport journey or chasing a new PR, we've got you. And if you don't see the answer here, post it in the Facebook group — the collective knowledge of hundreds of seasoned triathletes is at your fingertips.

Just getting started with triathlon? The best thing you can do is connect with other people interested in the sport. Join the club's Facebook Group — most rides, runs, and open water swims get organized right there. As a PTC member you'll also get discounts to events and members-only information.

PTC members are always happy to lend a hand and provide advice. Don't hesitate to ask questions — just don't be afraid of the cascade of answers you'll receive!

Want a mentor? New members can request one by contacting the PTC Board at pittsburghtriathlonclub@gmail.com.

Get Fit Families Open Water Swims — Porter's Cove, Lake Arthur, Moraine State Park. Mondays 5:00–7:00 PM, Sunday mornings 8:00 AM. Check schedule and pre-registration before you go.

Moraine State Park — Two designated beaches on Lake Arthur, open Memorial Day–Labor Day, sunrise to sunset, swim at your own risk.

  • Pleasant Valley Beach (South Shore): 1,200-ft turf/sand beach, paved water path, volleyball court, playground. Swim area ~120 yards.
  • Lakeview Beach (North Shore): 550-ft sand beach, swim area ~100 yards.
  • Amenities: showers, changing facilities, snack bars.
  • Member tip: go early on weekends to avoid crowds — usually clear before 10:30am.

Keystone State Park — Sand beach, late-May–mid-September, 8am–sunset, swim at own risk, food concession available. Member tip: mostly deep at the rope; ~150m per length, 5 laps = Olympic distance.

Raccoon Creek State Park — 500-ft ADA-accessible sand/turf beach, late-May–mid-September, 8am–sunset. Swim lane ~140 yards/length, 5–6 ft deep. Bathhouse and concession stand, 1–2 miles from the Route 18 or Route 30 entrances.

Pittsburgh's Three Rivers — Sara Quesen maintains an open water resource for swimming Pittsburgh's rivers at openwaterpghwordpress.com.

Cycling Clubs

  • Bike PGH — transforming Pittsburgh's streets into safe, accessible places to bike and walk.
  • Western Pennsylvania Wheelmen — founded 1969, one of PA's largest bike clubs, weekly rides.
  • Pittsburgh Major Taylor Cycling Club — bringing recreational cycling to Pittsburgh's inner-city neighborhoods.
  • Premier Bicycle Club of Western Pennsylvania — founded 2016, weekly rides + special events.
  • Mt. Lebanon Cycling & Caffeine Club — "mostly old guys in tight pants."
  • Pittsburgh Randonneurs — long-distance organized rides, 100–1200 km.

Terrain — Pittsburgh's hilly Appalachian foothill terrain runs roughly 1,000 ft of climbing per hour. Average speeds: ~18mph experienced, 13–14mph for newcomers on longer rides.

Sample Routes

  • North Hills (North Park): 5-mile lake loop; Visit the Alpacas; Cement Plant ride (112mi); North Park to Moraine
  • Cranberry/North: 50mi Cranberry loop; Farm Fresh Ice Cream route; Slippery Rock area ride
  • Downtown/City: 75mi Red Belt Loop; Washington Blvd Oval & Highland Park Loop; Loop Around the Burgh (35mi); Saxonburg/Deer Creek loop (49mi)

"Wear a helmet, or don't get on your bike." Train with others when you can, and consider a RoadID in case you're ever out alone.

If you're in an accident: Bike Pittsburgh provides crash-resource guidance, an annual crash map, and cycling clinics.

Practice spots: Bud Harris Cycling Oval for advanced skills; North Park and sections of the GAP trail are good for practicing clipping in/out.

Newer to cycling or open water and feeling anxious about it? The club is always looking for experienced members to mentor newcomers — connect via the Facebook group or at a group workout.

Do I need to already be a triathlete to join?
Not at all — PTC welcomes every level, from first-timers to elites. Request a mentor and someone will help you get oriented.

What does membership actually cost?
$50 individual, $80 family, $25 full-time student (18+) for the calendar year. See the Membership page for what's included.

Do I need my own bike/wetsuit/gear to start?
No — start with what you have. Group runs and many rides don't require special gear, and members are a great resource for borrowing or buying used equipment as you get more into the sport.

How do I find out about group workouts?
Most rides, runs, and open water swims get organized in the club's Facebook group — that's the fastest way to see what's happening this week.

I'm nervous about open water swimming or group rides — is that normal?
Very. The club actively pairs newer members with experienced mentors for exactly this — ask in the Facebook group or email the board.

How do I add my race to the race list?
Open the race list and add yourself directly to the shared spreadsheet — no approval needed.
